## Source-verified gap
`normalizeNetworkInventoryDocument` silently takes the first candidate of `Devices`, `Nodes`, `records` in JSON/Office multi-table input and skips other possible inventory tables. This can discard conflicting devices and make source-derived physical edges appear complete when two candidate tables exist. It also silently drops nonempty unknown source columns in supported Devices/CSV/TSV/JSON without telling the operator. Canonical `Metadata+Nodes` workbooks already have their own schema and cannot be silently combined with a competing Devices/records inventory. Existing P2c2 port/Engine truth, P2a manual competing-links, P2b/P2c1 reimport/replay and all five adapters must remain unaffected.
